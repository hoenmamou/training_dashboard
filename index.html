Infection Control & Incident Reporting

A narrated, self-paced competency course for Direct Support Workers: how infections spread and how to stop them, and what to do, who to call, and how to write it up when something happens.

Part of your required annual training. Louisiana licensing standards require every direct service worker in Home and Community Based Services to complete a minimum of eight hours of training each year to maintain competence, and to demonstrate that competence by examination. This course covers three required subject areas — infection control, critical incident reporting, and detecting and reporting abuse and neglect — and the final test is the competency check.
